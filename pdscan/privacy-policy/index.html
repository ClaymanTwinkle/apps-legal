PDScan Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

PDScan measures your pupillary distance on your device. This policy explains what the app does with data.

What the app processes

  • Camera and depth data. PDScan uses the front camera and the Face ID depth sensor (ARKit face tracking) to find your pupils and compute the distance between them. This processing happens entirely on your device, in real time. The camera image and face geometry are not stored, not saved to the photo library, and not sent anywhere.
  • Selfie on devices without Face ID. On a device without the depth sensor, you take a selfie with a card for scale and place markers on it. The photo is kept in memory only while you place the markers; it is not saved in the app or to the photo library and not sent anywhere.
  • Measurements. The numbers you choose to save (PD values, the date, the reading distance used, who it belongs to (Me or a name you type), and the steadiness of the reading) are stored in a file inside the app's own storage on your device. Deleting a measurement or the app deletes them.
  • Prescription and frame size. If you type your prescription (sphere, cylinder, axis, addition, prism) or the size of your current glasses for an order sheet, they are stored in the app's own storage on your device, per person, and used only to print the order sheet. You can remove them in the order sheet; deleting that person's measurements or the app deletes them too.
  • Purchases. The one-time unlock is handled by Apple's App Store. PDScan receives only the purchase status from Apple and stores a yes/no flag on the device.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No servers of our own; the app makes no network requests except those Apple's App Store framework makes for purchases.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • No use of face data for identification, authentication, advertising, profiling, or anything other than computing the measurement, in line with Apple's Developer Program License Agreement section 3.3.3(K).

Sharing

If you use the Share button, the app hands a short text with your PD values to the iOS share sheet. If you create an order sheet, the app makes a one-page PDF with your PD values (and the name, prescription and frame size, if you typed them) and hands it to the share sheet. Where either goes from there is your choice.

If you use Report a problem in Settings, the app first shows you the whole text: app version, device model, iOS version, the last error, and your five most recent measurements (PD, number of readings, spread and distance), with no name and no images. It leaves the device only if you email it from your mail app or paste it somewhere yourself.

Children

PDScan does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
