PDScan Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

PDScan measures your pupillary distance on your device. This policy explains what the app does with data.

What the app processes

  • Camera and depth data. PDScan uses the front camera and the Face ID depth sensor (ARKit face tracking) to locate the center of each eye and compute the distance between them. This processing happens entirely on your device, in real time. The camera image and face geometry are not stored, not saved to the photo library, and not sent anywhere.
  • Measurements. The numbers you choose to save (PD values, the date, the reading distance used, an optional name you type, and the steadiness of the reading) are stored in a file inside the app's own storage on your device. Deleting a measurement or the app deletes them.
  • Purchases. The one-time unlock is handled by Apple's App Store. PDScan receives only the purchase status from Apple and stores a yes/no flag on the device.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No servers of our own; the app makes no network requests except those Apple's App Store framework makes for purchases.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • No use of face data for identification, authentication, advertising, profiling, or anything other than computing the measurement, in line with Apple's Developer Program License Agreement section 3.3.3(K).

Sharing

If you use the Share button, the app hands a short text with your PD values to the iOS share sheet. If you create an order sheet, the app makes a one-page PDF with your PD values (and the name, if you typed one) and hands it to the share sheet. Where either goes from there is your choice.

Children

PDScan does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
